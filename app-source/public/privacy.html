Privacy Policy

James Fitness System is a fitness tracking and education product. You may use the local version without creating a cloud account. If Cloud Sync is enabled and you choose to sign in, the app transmits the data needed to synchronize your account over HTTPS to the configured Supabase project.

Data you may provide

Name, email address, profile data, weight and body measurements, nutrition logs, workout logs, recovery entries, notes, preferences and other fitness-related information you enter into the app.

How the data is used

Data is used to provide app functionality, personalize your fitness plan and show your own trends and recommendations. James Fitness System does not sell health or fitness data and does not use it for third-party advertising.

Local and cloud storage

Without Cloud Sync, your records stay in the browser or app storage on your device. With Cloud Sync, a copy is associated with your authenticated account in Supabase so it can be restored on another device.

Delete your account

If you created a Cloud Sync account, open Settings → Account & Sync → Delete account. This initiates permanent deletion of the cloud account and associated synchronized data. Local-only data can be deleted from Data Management.

Health and fitness data

The app is not a medical service and does not provide a diagnosis. Health and fitness information is used only to provide the app’s fitness-management features unless you explicitly choose a future integration that states otherwise.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the support contact published with the App Store listing.
